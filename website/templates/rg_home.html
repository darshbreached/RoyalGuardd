01  /  Ranks

Roblox ranks, mirrored in Discord.

Bind a Roblox group rank to a Discord role once. When a member runs /update, their Discord roles are brought in line with their rank in your group. Rank binds, group binds and staff levels are all set per server.

02  /  Features

Everything a unit runs on.

Each part is its own module, so a server only runs what it uses.

Verification

Members link their Roblox account through a guided flow. Staff get account-age and alt-account flags, and can override by hand.

/forceverify/unverify

Ranks and staff

Change Roblox ranks from Discord with logged embeds. Members request ranks with proof and staff approve in one click.

/setrank/acceptrequest

Moderation

Warnings, bans and mutes that DM the member with evidence and an appeal link. Automod, anti-nuke and anti-raid protect the server.

/warn/ban/mute

Operations

Run activity checks, announce server start-ups, handle training requests and track leave of absence.

/ssu/activitycheck

Community

Tickets, reaction roles, invite tracking, booster colour roles and an economy with a currency you name yourself.

/balance/daily/work

Your branding

Army name, crest, colours and currency are set per server. Panels carry your identity, not ours.

/setup
